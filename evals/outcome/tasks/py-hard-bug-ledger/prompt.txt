Bug report from the finance team, about the ledger package:

"Our month-end numbers don't add up. Three things we noticed:
 - the sum of the monthly report doesn't match the account balance for accounts that had refunds;
 - converted amounts are sometimes one cent lower than what our bank shows (e.g. 10.01 USD to EUR at 0.9 should be 9.01 EUR, we get 9.00);
 - on an account that spans a year with single-digit months, the statement lists September 2024 after October 2024, and the monthly report has its months in the wrong order too."

Find and fix the causes in the library. The documented behaviour in the docstrings is correct: rounding is to the nearest cent (halves round away from zero), amounts are signed (negative = money out), `monthly_summary` returns the net amount per month in chronological order with keys like "2024-09", and dates may be written without zero padding ("2024-9-5") and must still sort and group correctly. Add tests for what you fix.
